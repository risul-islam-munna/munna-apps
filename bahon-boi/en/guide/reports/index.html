Reports, charts and transaction lists

See profit, cash and balances for any day, month or date range — for one vehicle or all — and open the entries behind every number.

When to use it

At the end of a day or month, or when you want to compare vehicles or check a driver’s dues.

Where to find it

  • Bottom bar → Reports
  • +, menu or “See all” → Transactions

What you need

  • A period and, if you like, one vehicle.

Steps

  1. Open Reports and choose Daily, Monthly or Custom. Move with Previous / Next, or pick From and To dates.
  2. Choose All vehicles or one vehicle.
  3. Profit and cash movement appear in separate cards, with balances as of the end of the period (or today, if that is earlier).
  4. Open the income & expense chart to compare earned income and expenses as bars — by day, or by month for long periods. Tap a bar for its amounts; scroll sideways for long periods.
  5. Tap any total or row to see the entries behind it, then tap an entry to open it.
  6. Transactions lists Income, Received and Expenses separately, with a vehicle filter.

Result

You get a period summary you can trust, because every figure can be traced back to its entries.

Mistakes and exceptions

  • A backdated entry also changes older months — this is expected.
  • Reports cannot be exported or shared from the app in this version.

Correct or undo

  • If a figure looks wrong, tap it, open the entry behind it and correct that entry.
