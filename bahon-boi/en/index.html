Bahon BoiVehicle accounts app · Android

Every vehicle’s accounts, in one book.

See what each vehicle really earned, what drivers still owe, what you spent and what profit is left — on your phone, in Bengali or English, without internet.

For owners of buses, private cars, CNGs, auto-rickshaws, trucks, pickups and other vehicles, and for small transport businesses in Bangladesh.

Accounting, explained

Earned, received, due, profit and cash are different numbers

Bahon Boi never adds a driver’s payment as new income, and never works out cash from profit. Here is one week of one vehicle, with example figures.

Earned income
What the vehicle earned for its days — paid or not.
Money received
Cash or transfers that actually reached you. Settles income; never adds to it.
Still to collect
Income − money applied to it.
Expenses
Owner’s costs, on the day the work happened.
Money paid
Payments for expenses, on the day money left your hands.
Profit
Earned income − expenses.
Net cash movement
Money received − money paid. Not a cash balance.
Non-cash adjustments
“Adjust against rent” and rent waivers change dues without moving cash.

Backup and restore

Protect your records — set it up on day one

Records live on your phone. Backups go to a folder you choose on the phone, so copying that folder somewhere else is what protects you if the phone is lost.

What the app does

  • After a one-time setup (Android), a data backup about once a day
  • Photos weekly by default, or monthly, or only on demand
  • “Back up now” any time; the newest 30 backups are kept
  • Optional recovery key to encrypt backups

What you should do

  • Check “last backup” in Backup & Restore now and then
  • Recommended routine: copy the “BahonBoi Backups” folder to a computer or USB drive every week
  • Write the recovery key down and keep it away from the phone and the backups

Good to know

  • Normal backups are not encrypted
  • Lose the key and the app installation, and a protected backup cannot be opened
  • Restore replaces the phone’s records; it does not merge
  • Folder backup is not available on iPhone

Offline use and privacy

Your accounts stay with you

Everything you record works without internet. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting service in the current version, and your records are not sent to the developer. When the phone is online, the app only downloads a small public file to check for a newer version.

Being offline does not make a phone risk-free. Records — including drivers’ ID details — are only as safe as your phone and your backup files.

FAQ

Common questions

Where can I get the app?

Bahon Boi has not been published in any app store yet. When it is, the download link will appear on this page.

Is money from a driver added as new income?

No. Income is recorded once, as its own entry. When the driver pays, you record money received and link it to that income, so the due goes down. Money received never adds income, and you cannot apply more than an entry’s due — extra money must be kept as an advance.

Are daily rent and trip income recorded the same way?

No. For daily rent you enter a daily rate and the amount fills in as rate × days. For trip-based income you enter the actual amount earned and, optionally, the number of trips. Fixed contracts and other income are separate types too.

Which month does a late bill count in?

An expense counts in profit on the day the work happened; the payment counts in cash on the day you paid. If you enter something with an old date, reports recalculate.

Where are my records kept? Do I need an account?

No account is needed. Records are kept on your phone in the app’s own storage, and the app works without internet. Records are not sent to the developer.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Only what you backed up and copied off the phone can be restored. Set up Backup & Restore on day one, copy the “BahonBoi Backups” folder to a computer or USB drive regularly, and keep any recovery key somewhere else. Without a backup copy, records on a lost phone cannot be recovered.

Do I get a phone notification before a document expires?

Not yet. Expired and expiring documents appear inside the app — on the Home screen’s bell and alert strip and on the vehicle screen.

Can several owners share one vehicle?

Yes, as records: each owner’s contribution and share are kept. Owners do not get separate logins, and the app does not split profit between them.

Can I use Bahon Boi on a computer or online?

No. Bahon Boi is a mobile app. This website only explains it; you cannot keep accounts here.
