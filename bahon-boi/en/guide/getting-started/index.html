First steps, Home screen and settings

Bahon Boi keeps each vehicle’s income, money received, expenses, drivers and documents on your phone. It works without internet and needs no account.

When to use it

Read this first, right after installing the app, or when you want to try it safely with sample data.

Where to find it

  • Welcome screen → “Add your first vehicle” or “Try with sample data”
  • Bottom bar: Home · Vehicles · + · Reports · Settings
  • Menu (☰, top left) → App guide at the bottom

What you need

  • Nothing — you can start with sample data.

Steps

  1. Open the app. On the welcome screen tap “Add your first vehicle”, or open Vehicles and tap “Add vehicle”.
  2. Want to look around first? Tap “Try with sample data”, or turn on Demo mode in Settings. A banner shows while demo mode is on; tap “Exit demo” to go back to your own records.
  3. On the Home screen, choose a month with the month selector. Four cards show earned income, expenses, profit or loss, and money still to collect as of today.
  4. Below the cards you see money received and money paid in that month. Open “More figures” for net cash movement, unpaid expenses and advances. Tap any amount to see the entries behind it.
  5. Tap + in the bottom bar for quick actions: add income, add expense, money received, transactions, drivers and add driver.
  6. In Settings choose the language (বাংলা or English) and appearance (System, Light or Dark). The whole app, including its guide, switches language.

Result

Your first vehicle appears under Vehicles, and the Home screen starts filling in as you add entries. Demo data is kept in a separate place and never mixes with your own records.

Mistakes and exceptions

  • Every income and expense belongs to a vehicle, so add a vehicle before anything else.
  • Future months cannot be chosen on the Home screen.
  • Entries made in demo mode are sample data. Exit demo before recording real accounts.

Correct or undo

  • Turn off demo mode any time with “Exit demo”. Your own records were never changed.
