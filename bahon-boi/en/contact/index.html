Contact and support

Questions, a problem, or an idea for Bahon Boi? Email in Bengali or English.

Email

hello@munna.dev

Putting “Bahon Boi” in the subject helps.

Write an email

When reporting a problem, include

  • The app version (shown at the bottom of Settings and of the menu)
  • Your phone model and Android version
  • What you were doing, step by step
  • A screenshot if useful — with personal details covered

Please do not send

  • Your recovery key
  • Backup files or the app’s database
  • Photos of anyone’s NID, licence or other personal documents

They are never needed for support.
